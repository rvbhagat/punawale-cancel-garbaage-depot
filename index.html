COMMUNITY E-SIGN CAMPAIGN • PUNAWALE, PCMC

Protect Punawale’s Future

Demand to cancel the proposed garbage depot location at Survey Nos. 23 and 26, Punawale.
Residents are coming together to demand cancellation of the proposed location because of concerns about its proximity to educational institutions, the surrounding residential population, traffic and the potential long-term health and environmental impact.

Why we are raising this concern

🏫 Children & schools: Residents are concerned about the potential impact of waste-related odour, air quality, pests and other environmental factors on children and nearby educational institutions.
🏘️ Densely populated area: The proposed area is surrounded by a growing residential community and significant daily activity.
🌱 Environment & public health: A garbage facility can create long-term concerns relating to odour, waste handling, traffic, pests, air and water quality if not appropriately located and managed.
📍 Cancellation of proposed location: We demand that the proposed garbage depot at Survey Nos. 23 and 26 be cancelled and that this proposed location not be used for the garbage depot.

Our request

We demand that the PCMC and other concerned authorities cancel the proposed garbage depot location at Survey Nos. 23 and 26, Punawale. The proposed location is near schools and a densely populated area, and residents are concerned about potential long-term health, environmental, traffic, odour, waste-management and public-safety impacts.

This campaign is a community representation demanding cancellation of the proposed garbage depot location. It does not make a medical or environmental finding.
